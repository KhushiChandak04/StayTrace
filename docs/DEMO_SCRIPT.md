# 4-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem

"Room damage disputes are usually not about whether a scratch exists. They are about whether the scratch was already there. People have photographs, chats and paper checklists, but no structured evidence timeline."

## 0:30–1:15 — Move-in

Create Room 407. Load or capture the move-in scan. Show the images and the observation table.

## 1:15–2:00 — Move-out

Create the move-out scan. Run the comparison. Show the before/after pair and difference heatmap.

## 2:00–2:45 — Evidence timeline / claim

Enter: "The desk was damaged during the stay." Show the evidence-oriented response and emphasize that the application does not make a legal determination.

## 2:45–3:30 — Uncertainty

Show an uncertain finding and manual-review flag. Explain that the product separates visible observation, correspondence, inference and uncertainty.

## 3:30–4:00 — Snapdragon story

Open diagnostics. On the validated Snapdragon machine, show the real GenieX model, runtime, compute path and measured latency. Do not use numbers until they are measured on the target system.
